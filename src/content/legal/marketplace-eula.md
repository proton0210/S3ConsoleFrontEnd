AWS bills Marketplace orders under the Buyer’s AWS agreement and Marketplace terms. Renewal, cancellation, and refund handling follows the listing, the applicable order, and AWS Marketplace procedures. Cancellation or expiry of a Marketplace agreement can end or reduce the corresponding Serverless Buckets entitlement after any period stated in the listing or required by AWS. Cancellation does not delete data in the Buyer’s AWS account.

## 5. Customer AWS data and credentials

Serverless Buckets is a desktop client. Amazon S3 requests go directly from the Buyer’s desktop to the Buyer’s AWS account using AWS credentials and IAM permissions selected by the Buyer. ServerlessCreed does not proxy or store S3 objects. AWS access keys, secret keys, session tokens, and SSO tokens are not sent to ServerlessCreed.

The Buyer controls and is responsible for its AWS accounts, IAM permissions, resource configuration, backups, AWS charges, and all actions requested through the software. Details appear in the [Trust Center](/trust) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

Unless the applicable Marketplace listing or a separately signed agreement expressly states different commitments, support is provided on a best-effort basis by email at **vidit@serverlesscreed.com**, with no guaranteed response or resolution time and no contractual uptime percentage.
