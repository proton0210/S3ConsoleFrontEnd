Transmit 5 by Panic is a polished Mac file-transfer app that many people have used for years and are happy with. It connects to many kinds of servers and cloud storage, including Amazon S3. Buckets does one job: it is a desktop console for Amazon S3, CloudFront and S3-compatible storage.

## Choose Transmit if…

- **You move files between many kinds of servers**, not just S3. Buckets has no FTP support, and it can only mount SFTP, WebDAV and FTPS servers as a folder through rclone, not browse them in the app.
- **You want the most Mac-native feel.** Transmit is a long-standing Mac app with a devoted following.
- **You want a single low one-time price** and your S3 use is mostly uploading and downloading.
- **You have an Intel Mac.** Buckets currently ships for Apple silicon only.

## Choose Buckets if…

- **S3 is your job, not just a destination.** Buckets covers policies, lifecycle rules, Object Lock and retention, replication, Batch Operations, a security and PII scanner, Time Travel recovery from object versions, and IaC export to CDK, CloudFormation, SAM, or Terraform.
- **You work across many AWS accounts** with IAM Identity Center and want sessions that refresh themselves.
- **You run websites or downloads behind CloudFront.** You can publish a local folder to a private bucket with Origin Access Control and targeted invalidations. You can also sign URLs and cookies, and test and publish CloudFront Functions.
- **Your team isn't all on Macs.** The same license covers Windows and Linux; each seat includes 2 machines.
- **You want a lifetime option without a subscription.** Buckets' Lifetime license is $99 one-time, with future updates included.
